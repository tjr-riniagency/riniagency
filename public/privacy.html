Website Privacy Notice

Effective November 9, 2026

This notice explains how information is handled when you visit riniagency.com or submit a website inquiry. It covers this website; insurance applications, policies, and other services may have additional privacy notices.

Information you provide

Our inquiry form asks for your name, email address, phone number, the type of insurance you are interested in, whether you would like a policy review, and any message you choose to include. Please do not submit Social Security numbers, driver’s-license numbers, payment information, medical details, or other sensitive documents through this form.

How your information is used

We use your submission to respond to you, understand your insurance needs, follow up on your request, and maintain records of the inquiry. If you ask us to obtain insurance options or arrange coverage, relevant information may be shared with insurers, agencies, or other insurance partners involved in handling your request, as permitted by law.

Website and form providers

Our website is hosted through Cloudflare. Form responses are processed through Google Forms and stored in Google Sheets, with email notifications used to help us respond. These providers process information as part of delivering their services. Hosting providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, and request logs for site delivery and security.

You can read Google’s Privacy Policy and Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy for details about their practices. Links to other websites, including social media, are governed by those sites’ privacy practices.

Privacy questions and corrections

To ask how your website submission is handled, request a correction, or ask about removing it, contact TJ Rini at tjr@riniagency.com or 810-280-1845. We will review your request in light of applicable recordkeeping and legal obligations. Please do not include sensitive information in an initial email.

Security and updates

The website uses HTTPS to encrypt information in transit. No website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. We may update this notice as website practices change; the effective date above identifies the current version.

Insurance coverage requests

Submitting a website inquiry does not bind, change, or cancel insurance coverage. Coverage or changes take effect only after confirmation by an authorized agent or insurer. Coverage is subject to policy terms, conditions, underwriting, and availability.
